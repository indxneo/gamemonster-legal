GameMonster for Android Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

GameMonster helps you keep track of the video games, consoles and accessories you own and want. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We, the developer, never receive your collection. This policy covers GameMonster for Android phones and tablets; "your phone" below means whichever you use it on. GameMonster for iPhone and iPad has its own policy.

What GameMonster stores, and where

  • On your phone: your collection and wishlist, including prices, dates and notes you enter, your settings, recent searches, and automatic backups of your collection.
  • In your Google account, if Android's backup is on (you turn it on or off in your phone's system settings, not in GameMonster):
    • a copy of your collection, your settings and the backups on your phone, saved by Android about once a day;
    • it's stored by Google under your Google account and encrypted with your phone's screen lock, where your phone supports it;
    • we have no access to it. It's used to bring your collection back when you reinstall GameMonster or set up a new phone.
  • Files you export: backups and spreadsheets go only where you choose to save or share them.

What leaves your phone

  • Game artwork. Covers and screenshots load from IGDB (images.igdb.com, run by Twitch). IGDB can see your IP address and which images are requested, as with any website.
  • Game list updates. Once a day at most, GameMonster checks our update server for a newer game list, and downloads it only on Wi-Fi or another unmetered network. The request carries no information about you or your collection. The server, hosted by Cloudflare, sees your IP address, as with any website. We don't log or keep these requests.
  • Technical diagnostics from Google's on-device tools. Scanning uses Google's ML Kit, and Monster AI uses Gemini Nano through ML Kit, both running on your phone. ML Kit sends Google technical information about how these tools perform: your phone's model and Android version, the app's name and version, an identifier for this installation of the tools (not meant to identify you or your phone), timings, image sizes and error codes. It doesn't include your photos, your questions, the answers or your collection. Google uses it to keep ML Kit working and doesn't pass it to anyone else; see Google's ML Kit data disclosure.
  • Links you tap (price checks on PriceCharting or eBay, trailers on YouTube, IGDB) open in your browser or those apps. Those sites' own privacy policies apply.

What stays on your phone

  • Scanning. Photos and barcodes are read on your phone. When the text on a photo isn't enough, Gemini Nano may help read it, also on your phone. Photos are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Monster AI. On phones that have Gemini Nano, your questions, and the details of your games needed to answer them (such as titles, platforms, what's included, regions, wishlist prices and the total you've recorded spending), are answered by Gemini Nano on your phone, and nothing leaves it. If the model isn't on your phone yet, GameMonster downloads it from Google only when you tap to get it. GameMonster doesn't save your conversations: they're gone when you start a new chat or close the app. On other phones, Monster AI answers from your collection without an AI model.

What we collect

Nothing. GameMonster has no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own and no advertising. If you choose to share usage and diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Google Play may send us anonymous crash reports. They contain technical information about the crash, not your collection.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Delete All Data erases your collection, wishlist, backups, recent searches and settings from your phone. Android's next backup of GameMonster then replaces the copy in your Google account.
  • You can also turn off backup for your phone in system settings; Google then deletes the backed-up copy.
  • Deleting the app removes everything on that phone. Your Google backup copy, if any, stays until Google removes it or it's replaced.

Children

GameMonster doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
