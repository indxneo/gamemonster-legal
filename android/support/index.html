GameMonster for Android Support

Questions, problems or ideas: alishersv.dev@outlook.com. Please include your phone or tablet model and the app version (Settings › About › Version).

Keeping your collection safe

  • Google backup: with backup on in your phone's system settings, Android saves GameMonster's collection, settings and backups to your Google account about once a day (usually overnight, while charging on Wi-Fi). Deleting the app or getting a new phone then brings your collection back when you install GameMonster again with the same Google account. Settings › Backup › Google Backup opens the system settings.
  • Backups on your phone: GameMonster keeps copies on your phone: daily for a week, weekly for a month and monthly for a year. Restore any of them in Settings › Backups. They're deleted with the app, so export a backup file too if Google backup is off.
  • Backup file: Settings › Export › Backup File saves your whole collection as one file to your files, Google Drive, email or any app you choose.

Moving to a new phone

  • With Google backup on: set up the new phone with the same Google account and restore from your backup or copy data from your old phone, then install GameMonster. Your collection comes back with it.
  • Without it: on the old phone, go to Settings › Export › Backup File. On the new one, tap "Restore a Backup" on the welcome screen.

Moving between iPhone and Android

The backup file works both ways. On the iPhone, in GameMonster go to Settings › Export › Backup File and send the file to your Android phone; on Android, tap "Restore a Backup" on the welcome screen, or use Settings › Import › Backup File. From Android to iPhone, do the same the other way round. An iPhone's automatic on-device backups can't be read on Android; use Export.

Bringing a collection from another app

Settings › Import › From Another App reads spreadsheets from CLZ Games, GAMEYE, PriceCharting and most other apps. You'll see what matched before anything is added.

Monster AI

On phones with Gemini Nano (Google's on-device AI), Monster AI chats about your collection. Elsewhere it still answers whether you own a game, how many games you own and what's on your wishlist.

Deleting everything

Settings › Delete All Data erases your collection, wishlist, backups and settings from this phone. It can't be undone. Export a backup first if you might want it back.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and we never receive your collection.
